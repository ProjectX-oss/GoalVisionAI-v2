# Calibration Drift Governance

Calibration monitoring calculates Decimal Brier score, log loss, ECE, MCE, reliability bins, probability bias, over/underconfidence, and extreme-probability failure rate from settled observations. Results are evaluated globally and by market, model generation, and calibration artifact.

`CALIBRATION_DRIFT_WARNING` requests review; `CALIBRATION_DRIFT_BLOCKED` prevents publication after hysteresis; insufficient samples remain explicitly insufficient. A `RECALIBRATION_RECOMMENDED` record contains evidence and minimum validation requirements only. It never fits or activates a calibrator.

Calibration measures probability reliability, not betting profitability. Winning bets do not excuse sustained overconfidence, and losses alone do not prove bad calibration.
