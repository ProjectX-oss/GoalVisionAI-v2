# LAB odds policy

GoalVision LAB has no hard minimum decimal-odds floor for:

- prematch singles;
- individual three-leg combo legs;
- combined three-leg combo odds; or
- LIVE LAB selections.

A low price is neither a rejection reason nor evidence that a selection is
valid. Every LAB path remains fail-closed and requires a current valid price,
the applicable freshness and positive-value/edge checks, sufficient independent
signals, ensemble agreement, no material disagreement, final review and lineup
or context evidence where applicable. Three-leg combos still require exactly
three independently approved legs and retain correlation, disjoint-selection,
exposure, duplicate-publication and exactly-once protections. Existing upper
experimental safety limits are not minimum odds floors and remain in force.

Historical bookmaker odds are prohibited in current LAB selection. Only current
captured bookmaker prices with their source and timestamps may be used.

Official is a separate product. Its single, fallback-combo, publication,
bankroll, statistics and selection policies are unchanged, including its
minimum-odds rules.
