# Market, Competition, and Bookmaker Governance

All eleven canonical single markets remain supported. Each populated market is evaluated independently for settled volume, wins/losses/voids, Brier score, log loss, ECE, average odds, publication count, and blocker rate. Small scopes remain `*_SAMPLE_INSUFFICIENT`; one lucky obscure competition is never treated as validated.

Competition and bookmaker scopes use the same immutable cutoff. Odds governance monitors price distribution, stale quote rate, selected-market frequency, and concentration. A blocked market, competition, or bookmaker fails only the applicable LAB publication review unless system policy also requires a global pause. The preferred bookmaker is never changed automatically.

Odds and hypothetical returns are operational context, not model-performance proof. No bet or bookmaker transaction occurs.
